## The thing that makes both asks easy

Do not lead by asking them to release the mesh. **You do not need the mesh.**

`js/scene-penance.js` already lays down a planar UV projection matched to a
29.26 × 13.10 m plate. A single **orthographic render of the carved face**, taken
from the model they already have, at 2.234:1 aspect, satisfies it completely.
That is a five-minute export for them and gives away no geometry — a far smaller
ask, and a far likelier yes. Ask for that first; mention the mesh only as the
better-if-possible option.

## Notes before you send

- **Check the claims match your intentions.** I have described the site as open
  and non-commercial and promised on-panel credit with capture date and method.
  If any of that is wrong for how you plan to publish, fix it before sending —
  do not promise terms you will not keep.
- **Ask 1 is the one that gets answered.** If you only send one sentence, make it
  the orthographic-render request.
- Neither upload states a licence. "View-only by default" is Sketchfab's fallback,
  not a decision they necessarily made — which is why asking what the terms *are*
  is a reasonable, low-pressure opening in its own right.
- Théo Derory's field is medieval European building archaeology, so this capture
  is likely personal rather than part of his doctoral work. Do not assume it is
  research output.
