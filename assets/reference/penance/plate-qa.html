Two things this page does not show, and both must reach the visitor.
The plate is a mirror. In the model u = 0 is at the most-negative z, which is north. Facing an east-facing wall, north is on the viewer's right — and in this photograph the shrine (Fergusson's “southern half”) is on the left with the elephants (his “right half”) on the right, which agrees. So u increases right-to-left and the rectified image must be flipped horizontally before it becomes the plate.
Perspective is not corrected. One landmark gives translation, not a homography, so this is an affine fit only. The upper registers recede from the camera and stay that way; no source records the camera height or standoff that a real rectification would need.
